物語The story

Six hundred years ago, the Kagenui stitched a veil across the sky and sealed a whole country of exiles behind it. Now the weave is wearing thin. Rei, the last student of an old swordsman, follows her master down from the mountains and learns, chapter by chapter, who is holding the Veil closed — and why he means to cut it.

一

Read every strike

Deflect at the last moment to break your opponent's posture, then finish with a single decisive Execution. Perilous attacks must be jumped or dodged.

二

Five stances

Gale, Stillwater, Thunder, Shadow and Mirror. Each is earned in a trial and changes how you fight, hide or move.

三

Move like a shinobi

Wall-jumps, a dash, grappling rings, swimming, rising winds and, late in the journey, stepping between two sides of the world.

四

Mercy is a choice

Every champion after the first can be spared. Gather all 25 ink scrolls to unlock the third ending.

六章Six chapters

Each chapter is hand-built with its own place, mechanics, champion and stance, and each one can be replayed for a better grade.

入手Download

Free. No installer, no account. Unzip and play. A controller is recommended; keyboard and mouse work too.

Windows

Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit.

Download for Windows
  • Unzip, then run ShadowVeil.exe.
  • Saves: %APPDATA%\XSO\Shadow Veil

Linux

Any 64-bit desktop from the last few years. Runs natively on Wayland and X11.

Download for Linux
  • tar xf ShadowVeil-*-linux-x64.tar.gz, then ./ShadowVeil/shadow-veil
  • Saves: ~/.local/share/shadow-veil

All releases, checksums and release notes are on GitHub.
